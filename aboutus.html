Company history

From hit show to Las Vegas landmark.

Gordon Ramsay Hell's Kitchen opened at Caesars Palace in January 2018 as the world's first restaurant inspired by the internationally recognised television series.

The restaurant translates the show's most recognisable elements into a physical dining experience. Red and blue kitchen details, the fiery pitchfork, an open kitchen, and views of the Las Vegas Strip create a setting that is both theatrical and distinctly connected to the brand.

Its menu brings together dishes associated with the show and Gordon Ramsay's wider culinary career, including Pan-Seared Scallops, Lobster Risotto, Beef Wellington, and Sticky Toffee Pudding.
